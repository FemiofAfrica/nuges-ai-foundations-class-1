01 · Observe

Read the market before writing the campaign

Live evidence sprint in Chrome or the team browser. Collect about 5 to 10 signals only: 3 comparable agencies or competitors, 3 public campaign or channel examples, 3 audience or category signals.

Campaign exercise parameters

Positioning: NUGES as a forward-thinking and innovative media and advertising company.

07 · Final synthesis

Validation, permissions and approval

Apply these controls across the full Class 2 journey and ordinary day-to-day tools. Classroom outputs remain drafts and internal work only.

Validation

Is each claim traceable to approved NUGES context, approved public material or [NUGES TO CONFIRM]? Are audience insight, pain point, proposition, big idea, channel role and CTA consistent? Hold unsupported proof, invented audience assertions and unverified competitor comparisons.

Permissions

Assign who can view, edit, comment on and approve the knowledge base, strategy document, review draft and execution table. Identify material restricted from broad sharing. Access is not approval for external action.

Approval roles

Context owner · Strategy lead · Creative lead · Designated external-release approver. Assign the actual people during the build. No work moves to external action without documented designated approval.
