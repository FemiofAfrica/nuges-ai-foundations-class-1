00Class contract

The room creates two connected systems with different ownership and permissions.

Layer one

Shared NUGES organizational context

Institutional knowledge, verified facts, authorized internal facts, definitions, voice decisions, audiences, services, workflows, examples, constraints, decision rules, data boundaries, and approval gates.

It needs sources, owners, sensitivity, permission, and review dates.

Layer two

Personal context engine

A participant's NUGES role, responsibilities, preferences, goals, recurring tasks, output formats, working style, and personal quality rules.

It references shared NUGES truth and remains private unless explicitly shared.

Facilitator says

“We build the shared NUGES organizational system first. Only after that does each person build a personal context engine. Shared truth and personal preference must stay connected without being confused.”

Live NUGES discovery sequence
Step 1 of 4

Collect candidate knowledge

Femi asks the room and the authorized NUGES representative what an AI assistant needs to know. Capture identity, voice, audiences, services, workflows, constraints, examples, decisions, data boundaries, and approval gates before debating wording.

Prompt: “Name the item. We will label it before we use it.”

Step 2 of 4

Separate evidence from gaps

Use four labels: Verified public, Authorized internal, Proposed, and [NUGES TO CONFIRM]. Record source, owner, sensitivity, permission, review date, and AI use for every item.

Rule: A plausible answer is not a NUGES fact.

Step 3 of 4

Validate with authority

Ask who can confirm each internal claim. If the representative or approved material is not available, leave the field [NUGES TO CONFIRM]. Record conflicts as decisions to resolve, not as blended wording.

Artifact: NUGES Organizational Context Index and Sources and Decisions page.

Step 4 of 4

Turn context into rules

Convert validated knowledge into a voice card, audience card, services and workflow map, constraints, examples register, decision rules, data boundaries, approval gates, and a task brief.

Artifact: Shared NUGES Organizational Context v1.

06Data governance and permissions

Classify

Public, internal, confidential, restricted

Classify every item before sharing. Keep restricted material out unless an explicit approved process permits it.

Permission

Retrieve, draft, retain, action support, prohibited

Record the exact permission. Drafting never grants authority to send, publish, delete, buy, or change records.

Least privilege

Limit tools and connections

Review permissions before connecting an API, MCP server, WebSocket channel, or action tool. Use only the access required.

Review

Human approval is visible

Require a named human gate for external claims, client commitments, publication, regulated content, disclosure, and irreversible actions.

Stop condition

Pause when source, owner, sensitivity, permission, or approver is unclear. Keep personal context private by default. [NUGES TO CONFIRM] is a valid result. Guessing is not.

Proposed NUGES operating rules

  1. Label every NUGES-specific claim as Verified public, Authorized internal, Proposed, or [NUGES TO CONFIRM].
  2. Record source, owner, sensitivity, permission, and review date.
  3. Ask when a required fact is missing or conflicting.
  4. Keep credentials, private personal data, confidential client material, and restricted files out of unapproved AI tools.
  5. Require human review before external use or irreversible action.
  6. Retain only approved, useful, current memory with a review or expiry date.
  7. When output fails, revise the context, prompt, tool, feedback, or guardrail and retest one variable.

Status: Proposed workshop draft, not verified NUGES policy.
